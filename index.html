Why We Rise

Every Person Deserves
To Live Free.

Rise for the Silenced is a national anti-trafficking coalition united by one fundamental belief: Freedom, dignity, and justice belong to every person.

We envision a world where survivors are heard, communities are equipped to recognize exploitation, and people working to end human trafficking are stronger together.
